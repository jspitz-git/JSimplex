DSE weight FTRAN -> replacement with the original direction. This plan is a
prospective qualification specification, NOT launch authorization or implementation.
S3-C source findings must pass independent review first. Do not reopen HH bootstrap,
controller-budget audits, or rerun S3-A/B/mode-selection or generic lifecycle tests.

First perform ONE bounded offline preparation: select a tiny existing S3 fixture
(no new LP family), freeze Float64 inputs and exact reference equations, and bind
the precise route-control seam. If forcing the two operation modes requires global
method replacement or changing production arithmetic, reject that design. A private
source clone must have exact inverse and only local route-control substitutions;
no method overwrite. Preserve all ordinary checks and preparation eviction.

Target at most ten independent component sequences: five public managers, two
requested mixed directions (entering sparse/weight dense and entering dense/weight
sparse). HH records actual dense fallback, never calls it an indexed HH solve.
Use one pre-existing update per sequence. Freeze constructor/update/solve/refactor
attempt totals BEFORE any execution, including setup, internal update BTRAN,
orientation validation bounds and policy corrections. No guessed large caps.

limitation rather than tuning after a solve. Native backend only in this narrow
Float64 qualification; other precisions/backends retain explicitly limited reuse.

tau, original direction, basis/history and prepared payload/identity as applicable.

bound. Include a zero direction coefficient whose finite positive old weight is

component stress input, not proof of reachable public pricing state. Do not alter
production floor, tolerance or precision. Empty/full support qualification already
exists at the producer and is not repeated as a new census.
