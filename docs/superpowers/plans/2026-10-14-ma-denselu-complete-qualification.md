This is justified by the actual guard source change and MA-V's accepted zero-allocation result, not by an automation tick or an assumption that Rational improved. The old 52-case run used a different candidate and remains rejected48/52.

A Rational failure rejects the component qualification even if it is small and events are equal. No repeated attempts to obtain a favorable allocator history.
