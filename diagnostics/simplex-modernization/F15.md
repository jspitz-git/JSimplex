# F15 — Partial pricing with full-scan certification

Status: verified. Complete production/development suites and all twelve preset
measurement groups completed. Parent: `8a9d54b` (F14).

## Behavior

Adaptive strategy uses an owned candidate pool when the pricing domain has
more than 128 entries. The block size is 64; the threshold avoids pool overhead
for domains no larger than two blocks. This structural choice predates corpus
measurements. Legacy strategy retains full pricing. The internal
`partial_pricing=false` policy provides an independent ablation.

Primal pools hold variable indices; dual pools hold candidate basic rows.
Each selection rechecks current states, fixed/free bounds, reduced costs or
bound violations, and pricing weights. A new best candidate outside a nonempty
pool may wait. Scores are never retained across pivots. Cyclic block scans
refill an exhausted pool, but returning zero requires a complete scan of the
current domain within that call. Blocks inspected before a pivot do not count
toward a terminal certificate. Full scans break ties by the smallest index,
including after cursor wraparound.

Normal pivots and bound flips advance basis generation while retaining indices
for revalidation. Recomputations, actual working-cost changes, perturbations,
phase changes, and recovered bases invalidate the pool and require a fresh full
scan. Pricing-weight rejection and numerical pivot rejection do likewise.
A dual infeasibility proof requires a complete current scan. The shared driver
still independently recomputes and certifies the original model with unchanged
user tolerances and the same time/completed-step budget.

Pivot and recovery trials own their pool vectors and history. Cancelled trials
cannot publish a cursor or candidate list. Diagnostic scan-work counters record
actual attempts through the shared diagnostics dictionary, including discarded
attempts; they are aggregates, not solver-state observer events. Full-pricing
baselines also record scanned/scored entries and full passes. These counters
cover candidate selection, not every vector pass in the solver: feasibility
checks, weight validation, and updates may still visit full vectors.

Partial pricing retains each explicit rule's score arithmetic. Automatic
pricing uses F14's exponent-scaled floating dual scores and widened exact
scores. No tolerance-based omission of improving candidates is introduced.

## Preset measurements

Two arms use the same F15 source, adaptive strategy, automatic pricing, and
partial pricing disabled/enabled. All other implemented features remain on;
clock-based refactorization is off. Each solve uses Float64, PFI, native
refactorization, 100000 iterations, 60 seconds, and 24576 MiB requested memory.
Workers perform one uninstrumented warmup and, for diagnostic samples,
one additional instrumented warmup. MIPLib inputs are explicit LP relaxations.

NetLib adlittle receives seven diagnostic-free samples per method/arm; the
preselected wide external case scsd8 receives three. The diagnostic/kernel
census includes scsd8, degen2, MIPLib markshare_4_0, and the generated
wide-bound129 calibration, once per method/arm. Presolve is disabled only for
the calibration. This gives 56 fresh observations in twelve groups, without
raw parent reuse. The calibration is mechanism evidence, not an independent
external holdout. The degeneracy check extends beyond pk1 to degen2 and
markshare_4_0. Unfinished runs and unavailable/inconclusive mapped dual
witnesses are retained below.

No full runtime, medium, or oversized solves are part of this feature's preset
measurements. Future runtime runs retain 360 seconds per solve and the
24576 MiB memory request.

## Interpretation and retained limits

All 40 optimal observations passed original-model primal certification and had
consistent original objectives. All four numerical errors and twelve time limits
belong to scsd8. Neither arm solved that model: disabling partial pricing ends
primal runs in exhausted feasibility recovery; enabling it continues to the time
limit. Dual runs reach the limit in both arms. The scsd8 timing rows describe
unfinished runs and must not be interpreted as time-to-optimum speed ratios.
Fewer refactorizations and domain visits are useful work observations, not new
solved-LP coverage.

The seven-sample adlittle primal median regresses by about 16%, with more steps
and refactorizations; its dual path is unchanged. On degen2, the disabled arm
exactly reproduces F14's automatic-pricing step/refactorization counts and
objective. Partial pricing reduces primal refactorizations from 588 to 289 and
its single diagnostic time from 23.43s to 19.23s, but increases dual
refactorizations from 74 to 363 and diagnostic time from 9.08s to 9.62s. These
single diagnostic observations are not a repeated timing study. Markshare's
small domains retain full pricing and identical work in both arms.

The generated wide calibration retains its analytic optimum -129 and all 129
primal flips. Domain visits fall from 17030 to 1104, full scans from 131 to 8,
with 122 pool hits. Candidate-selection kernel time nevertheless rises from
about 70 to 94 microseconds in this one observation: pool management has a cost.
Its zero-step dual solve does not establish a dual-pool speed benefit. Candidate
pools can change pivot paths and later automatic pricing decisions, so the
ablation includes those effects, not just scan-loop replacement.

Eleven mapped dual witnesses are available: eight have zero sign and
complementarity error. Three degen2 witnesses retain strict infinite
complementarity diagnostics for multipliers pointing toward absent bounds;
their sign errors are 8.79e-14 (disabled dual), 6.54e-13 (enabled primal), and
9.45e-13 (enabled dual). The other 45 observations explicitly lack a mapped dual
witness. These diagnostics are not silently rounded away or presented as exact
KKT certificates.

Observed process peak RSS ranges from 1586839552 to 1988243456 bytes; this is a
process high-water mark, including warmup. The effective requested address-space
allowance is 25141338112 bytes. Per-sample allocated bytes, GC/compilation time,
input hashes, policy snapshots, and kernel/event counts are retained in
[F15-results.json](F15-results.json).

The F15 mechanism and degeneracy checks pass, including degen2 and the explicit
LP relaxation of markshare_4_0. Combined with F12–F14's perturbation/pricing
ablations, this closes the planned stage verification without increasing the
iteration limit. Performance remains mixed and scsd8 remains unsolved. Partial
pricing stays within the opt-in adaptive strategy, with its independent internal
switch for ablation; the public legacy default is unchanged. No broad speedup,
new external solved coverage, or final rollout is claimed.
