## Behavior

A primal bound flip uses the reliable, unoriented tableau column to update
basic values by `x_B -= signed_change * column` and move the entering variable
to its opposite bound. The basis, factorization, costs, reduced costs, and
pricing weights remain unchanged. The direct helper owns no iteration count;
the transactional driver publishes one completed step.

The helper predicts finite values before writing, rejects overlapping value
and direction storage, and treats a zero change as a no-op. Both the ratio
calculation and application retain stored BigFloat precision. The incoming
direction is checked even when incremental updates are enabled independently
of the other adaptive switches.

Every 20 completed steps, a flip triggers an independent recomputation and
comparison against its predicted values. A failed audit rejects the candidate
and enters the existing bounded refresh/recovery path. Pivot iterations still
perform a full recomputation; incremental pivots belong to F09. Original-model
certification and the F07 feasibility driver remain in place.

The staged path skips its former unconditional recomputation for flips, while
preserving deadline checks before publication and complete state before
completion observers run. Legacy steps keep their existing numerical path.
`incremental_primal=false` provides an F08 ablation over the F07 adaptive core.

validation, plus **28** separate paired adlittle observations:
[F08-results.json](F08-results.json). The main comparison measures the original baseline,
F07 adaptive, current legacy, F08 ablation, and current adaptive on the fixed
quick corpus, seven repetitions with diagnostics disabled. A separate census
enables diagnostic counters and kernel timing. MIPLib cases are LP relaxations.

All five quick profiles passed **112/112** solves; the diagnostic census
passed **16/16**. Legacy retains the preceding legacy path, and F08 ablation
exactly retains F07 adaptive objectives, iteration counts, and refactorizations.

in one process, reversing order on alternate repetitions and collecting garbage

corpus-wide speedup.

matrix, row upper bounds 2, and costs -1. Its known optimum is all ones, with
objective -256. Presolve is disabled to retain 256 actual simplex flips.

separate ablation/adaptive censuses count saved solves using the same updated
instrumentation. F07 kernel counters omitted some checked solves, so their
raw counts are not used as the operation-count reference.
