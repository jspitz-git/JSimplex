## Algorithm and certification

The correction variables contain both structural variables and original row
activities. With `v = [x; a]` and `M = [A, -I]`, the model uses primal residual
`-M*v`, dual residual `[c; 0] - M' * y`, shifted bounds, and independently bounded
power-of-two primal and dual scales. Activity costs include the multiplier term;
the correction solver's additional activities are fixed at the scaled residual,
which can be nonzero.

Residuals and correction additions use at least 128 bits and at least 64 guard
bits beyond the stored working data. Correction coefficients deliberately round
to the original working precision. Factors, simplex decisions, and updates stay
at that original precision throughout a correction solve. Wider residuals are
not counted as precision boosting. Rational solves never enter floating recovery.

A candidate must improve at least one primal, dual-sign, or complementarity error
by a factor of two without materially worsening another. Acceptance evaluates
the representable original-type point. The next round rehydrates that accepted
projection for wide residual evaluation, so conservative original-type stagnation
can request boosting before a wider internal iterate would stagnate. Final
certification retains the original
model and tolerances, checks nonbasic states and basic stationarity, and validates
a newly factored projected basis. An uncertain projection cannot create a
checkpoint. A typed private final witness lets diagnostics evaluate the accepted
multiplier without replacing it with a solve in an older inaccurate factor.

Correction failure or stagnation uses the remaining F23 budget when boosting is
enabled. A correction's infeasibility or unboundedness is never an original-model
terminal proof. Deadlines, completed iterations, and attempted factorizations
remain cumulative, including rejected corrections and failed factor attempts.
Memory estimation precedes correction-model allocation. Callback exceptions
retain their identity, and owned cost/bound perturbations are restored.

## Diagnostics

`lp_refinement` counts attempted rounds. `phase_lp_refinement` identifies their
time and steps, including the correction solver's internal feasibility phases.
`lp_correction_certification` identifies auxiliary certificates; only an accepted
original-model probe publishes `certification`. Linear corrections and actual
precision boosts retain their separate counters. Benchmark witness unscaling
preserves stored multiplier precision independently of actual factor precision.

Two narrow cold calls separate nested correction simplex and optional precision
boosting from LP orchestration. Automatic recovery has its own typed cold entry,
so ordinary solves do not infer the complete recovery graph. JET permits runtime
dispatch only at those exact methods; direct numerical kernels remain strict.

## Verification

Targeted functional tests cover the exact rational formulation, ranged/fixed/free
bounds, nonzero activity costs and extra fixed activities, representability,
original precision, all four update methods and both backends, callback errors,
shared limits, perturbations, and public primal/dual entries with objective
sense/constants, scaling and presolve. Targeted interpretation checks and native
compilation checks are recorded separately in the work ledger.

Native focused regression passed 736/736 checks in 6m54.3s. An additional
public-start probe of the ill-conditioned binary replay matrix still reports
NUMERICAL_ERROR in both algorithms, both with boosting alone and with LP
refinement plus boosting. A supplied structural basis and an automatic public
start are distinct coverage; the replay does not establish universal recovery.

The complete native production suite passed 253,612/253,612 checks in 122m55.5s.
The development suite passed 1,144/1,144 checks, including 249 JET checks. A final
audit verified the unchanged production source and both complete test inventories.

The fresh-basis replay completed seven samples per policy after two complete
observer warmups. Linear refinement alone returned NUMERICAL_ERROR in all seven
runs. Boosting, with or without LP refinement, returned seven independently
certified optima at working levels 53, 128 and 256 bits. LP refinement attempted
two rounds before boosting; it did not remove the need for higher precision on
this replay. Median times were 0.4694 ms for boosting and 0.4853 ms for LP
refinement plus boosting, including fresh initialization and recovery work.
