## Computational work and retained memory

No unnecessary new production work or memory cost was found. The evidence is the exact whole-source inverse and current-master call graph: new adapter work is one constant-size enum field plus constant-size forwarding/rejection at setup/copy/refactor; new full-vector/matrix scans, basis solves, coefficient copies/conversions and factorization calls are zero in solves, updates, pricing candidates, recovery and final certification. No dimension- or update-chain-dependent cache was added. The constructor-only function keyword is neither retained nor visited by the solve/update path. Actual allocation/dispatch effects have not been measured.

Existing setup/refactor costs remain material and must be charged later: one ordinary backend construction, native L/U extraction and validation, at most one scale-orientation solve with its vector/matrix work, optional staged U normalization, packed-U copies and new manager scratch. Native extraction is proportional to n plus exported nonzeros; Markowitz extraction also traverses the trailing dense core. Whole direct refactor constructs a replacement manager, unlike ordinary reusable spare/buffer paths. Copies retain shared read-only lower/permutation/scaling data and private scratch/upper/history contracts; fallback copies keep the ordinary backend copy ownership rules. These pre-existing costs were not optimized away or represented as savings.

Diagnostic snapshots, exact oracles and 20-element control copies are bounded correctness-test costs. They are not representative timing-window work. No timing or allocation benchmark was run because Task 1 expressly prohibits Julia execution; there is no speedup claim. Focused sparse/dense measurements and complete construction/refactor/extraction/retained-memory windows remain required at Task 3.
