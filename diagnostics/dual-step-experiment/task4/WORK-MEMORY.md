## Flat recovery

One run_phase while loop owns stages Normal/CorrectRow/CorrectDirection/RebuildBasis/Reprice/ResetDevex/LegacyBoundary/Terminate and a scalar pending failure. recover! performs one action, never recursively invokes whole phase/dual step. Candidate/row rejection limits are8per generation/row; correction limits up to3per failed equation/round, rebuild/reprice rounds at most2per stuck decision. Rebuild changes generations/rejection metadata, never resets episode rebuild counts; only a committed pivot resets the episode. Monotone total attempt/write/undo/refactor/correction/event counters are never rolled back.

Row correction computes complete B' residual/scales, directly performs one same-precision BTRAN correction and rechecks original criteria; corrected row discards/recomputes pricing/ratio/direction, without another unit BTRAN. Direction/BFRT correction computes complete original-B residual/scales, directly forwards one same-precision FTRAN correction and rechecks; entering/row dependencies are retained, BFRT correction retains entering output. Dense residual and finite/support capture passes are declared repair work. A completely assembled finite residual is required before solving; partial/overflowed residuals cannot be mistaken for a zero equation. Backend foreign exceptions propagate; output-capture rejection is distinct from backend throw handling.

Rebuild assembles current committed B once, attempts native refactor before setting validity, then stages primal recompute (1FTRAN), cost BTRAN and full pricing in owned proposal arrays. Every attempt counts including throws. Full source/nonbasic/basis coefficient loops, m RHS/captures, complete equation checks and O(N+z) pricing are counted cold repair work, not ordinary savings. A failed repair leaves committed numeric vectors unchanged; no partially repaired point is exported. Devex reset is once per rebuilt basis; the following full classification pass restores exact membership. Cost-only restoration stages authoritative working-model objective and prices, needs1BTRAN but no unrelated FTRAN or factor rebuild/Devex reset. Fixed-generation/factor binding guards staged repair publication.

Configured update ceiling and64double-zero-step monitor request synchronized maintenance before further solves/proposals; terminal/iteration-limit decisions do not force an unnecessary maintenance factorization. The original clock/start/phase offsets and local remaining iteration option are retained. Exhaustion allows at most one validated working workspace boundary or honest no-payload termination; no original certificate, actual legacy solve or fallback/reentry loop. A timeout never repairs a damaged factor merely to return a result. Real native initial/refactor/reprice internal work remains distinct from observed producer/attempt counters; no complete hidden-native-allocation/free ledger.
