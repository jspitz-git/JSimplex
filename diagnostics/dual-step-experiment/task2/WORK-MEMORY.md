# Task2 work, ownership and numerical scope

No public/default solver routing or production src/test is changed. E remains opt-in, with no dual pivot/ratio/commit/recovery controller. Kernel behavior is not a whole-solve or speedup claim.

## Owned receipts and storage

Task1 buffers/sets/factor owners are retained. KernelState adds3 solve receipts; each owns m Float64 saved RHS values and m Int RHS support IDs (48m bytes total payload on64bit), with logical used length. Fixed owner/generation/ticket/unit-row/factor-object-ID/validity records bind an actual output owner and RHS. No caller RHS reference or old factor graph is retained; factor identity is scalar, generation invalidation remains mandatory on any owned factor mutation/replacement. Three saved RHS/evidence slots are required because entering direction and aggregate BFRT displacement may coexist. Receipt/frame and ten counters are O(1); no dictionaries, snapshots or growing logs in a kernel.

Engine state and numeric arrays remain private; working CSC is immutable for the ownership epoch. Selected scratch identities and matrix identity are checked before use. Dimensions and alias checks reject overlapping destination, factor work, protected model/numeric state and index metadata. An internal saved RHS may be passed by exact owner identity; overlapping views are rejected. Unknown direct scratch/index/model mutation requires invalidation and discarding the phase, followed by fresh import/reconstruction from an unmodified source. rebuild_invariants! repairs only committed numeric/basis membership; it cannot repair poisoned support, scratch, RowAccess or immutable model metadata. No such unknown writer may continue through numeric rebuild alone. Scalar generations do not prove arbitrary external immutability.

Every source writer invalidates proposal/dependent evidence. The per-slot ticket changes at each RHS production; output support is certified only after a complete finite capture. Global value generation, slot ticket, exact owner/vector and current factor identity must all match. A factor exception invalidates all evidence and marks the factor invalid; a numeric invariant rebuild alone cannot repair it. Ordinary native calls count before forwarding, including throws. Foreign exceptions propagate unchanged. Stops share the original clock.

## Work charged

- Initial PFI remains the unchanged dense native forward/transpose solver. Copy/finite-check each RHS once, retaining its exact-zero support and unit classification during that pass; capture every dense output once with finite/support checking, IDs in ascending order. These O(m) passes are explicit costs, not sparse-factor savings. Failure-only dense scratch cleanup is bounded and declared.
- RowAccess is the once-built Task1 index; q is the sum of adjacency lengths for current rho support. q<=nnz(A)/4 selects indexed row pricing, otherwise original-order CSC; nonordered CSC uses dense fallback. Sparse accumulation visits all selected adjacency terms and tracks exact cancellation; both routes write/mark support without a second full output scan or per-call index/sort.
- BTRAN row validation deliberately calls the original complete `_dual_row_residual_ratio` once. Its call counter is recorded, but internal term counts are not instrumented; no claim that pricing eliminated this matrix pass.
- FTRAN direction residual visits nonzero direction coordinates in ascending basis order and all their stored CSC entries, plus the saved nonzero RHS. It records a marked union of every visited row, including canceled-to-zero residuals/scale terms; no compaction of that union precedes the finite/componentwise check. Omitted zero direction terms contribute only exact zero with finite input coefficients. Absolute scale and original zero/pivot/256eps criteria are retained. Nonfinite input/scale/residual or pivot is rejected conservatively.
- A caller-supplied external RHS to check_direction is compared to the saved RHS and charged O(m); the private ordinary path can pass the exact saved owner and reuse producer support. A separate unindexed component entry visits all RHS rows explicitly; it does not clobber the BFRT receipt.
- Clearing tracked scratch uses its previous logical prefix; stamp wrap costs O(d). No physical shrinking. Scalar alias/owner checks and fixed counters add constant per-operation overhead, included in later measurement. Sparse/dense choice applies to each pricing call, never permanently disables metadata.

Task1's deferred inverse zeros+fill setup pass is unchanged, not repaired as unrelated scope. No allocation/native-retention or performance measurements occur here. Required tests cover n8/n64 sparse/band/dense, reference equations, both pricing modes on one input, complete residual scales, tiny/signed-zero/cancellation/nonfinite data, alias/provenance, failed-attempt accounting and stopped calls. Injected test-only factor types prove forwarding/error accounting, not real native rollback/recovery. Production tolerances and safeguarded terminal certificates are unchanged.
