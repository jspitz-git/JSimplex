Timing uncertainty matters. Some cell medians exceed one: 107/480 A cells (all positive/zero), 90/600 B cells, 43/480 AB cells and 54/480 C cells. Individual windows have substantially wider ratios, particularly with GC. For example the representative sparse C cell has paired-ratio MAD 0.070 and ratio range 0.879–1.043; dense C has median 0.989, MAD 0.048 and a GC-inclusive maximum ratio 5.721. B whole-quality effects are often near timing noise while its allocation savings and full-finalizer improvement are consistent. Acceptance rests on source-preserved correctness plus repeated whole-call byte/event savings and representative timings, not on every timing improving or unchanged allocations alone.

Non-BigFloat controls retain equal allocation-event counts in every measured pair. Float32/64 and Rational{Int64} also retain equal bytes in every pair. Rational{BigInt} cell median candidate-minus-original byte differences span −880 to +1,360 bytes (24/32 cell medians differ); raw byte differences occur in 268/288 pairs across all 32 cells, absolute 16–59,200 bytes. This allocator-traffic variation is observed, not scientifically attributed to a kernel change. The exact generic Rational source is unchanged and event counts agree; no new Rational method exists. Control elapsed times are noisy: calls range down to 0.2 microseconds, with a Float32 cell median ratio as high as 1.852, and RationalBigInt up to 1.153. No speedup, exact byte invariance for BigInt controls, or universal absence of timing regression is claimed.

Computational work and retained memory: I found no unnecessary added production scan, matrix/vector copy, conversion, basis solve, repeated invariant check or dimension-dependent numeric cache. Dense term work remains O(n^2), sparse work O(nnz), and each finalizer O(n), with the same five finalizer traversals. Audit work remains the same copies and recomputation/certification solves; changing problem dimensions or update-chain length does not add a new retained structure. A retains only private product/magnitude scalars; B skips eager positive negations and exact zero/one constructors; C changes q unit constructions to at most one during q finite norm entries and uses the same magnitude sites. Scalar liveness is bounded O(1). We have no direct per-variant retained-live-memory instrument; process RSS cannot establish such a saving. Existing precision scans, wide scratch, numerical guards and audit copies are intentionally retained.

Numerical and coverage limits remain those stated in source admission: exact value/sign/stored-precision/return flags and symbols, partial output state, owner identity, exceptions and surrounding context were compared across BF64/128/256 and five rounding modes, with signed zero, NaN/Inf, extremes, mixed-stored512, view, alias and virtual price-audit controls. Custom-wrapper use and injected callback/recompute-unreliable branches are not claimed as executed coverage; they rely on unchanged source/boundary review. Audit malformed-label no-ops make no malformed-workspace guarantee. The benchmark uses small bases 4/16 and structural N32/96, no long update chains or full application trajectories, and does not qualify full-suite/x64. Permanent production correctness/cost regressions, exact-source staging and independent branch/actual-commit/master gates are still required before integration.
